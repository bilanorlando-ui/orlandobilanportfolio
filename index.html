03
I saw a better way

The Client Masterboard

When I started at Brandified, I was asked to create a client sheet that the owner and I could use to review the status of our clients. I could have just created a basic spreadsheet, but I decided to see how much more I could do with Monday.com.

I researched its automations, formulas, and other features and built a Client Masterboard that gave us a much clearer view of the entire client lifecycle. It tracked whether a client was active, on pause, or cancelled, their location and timezone, contract type, payment amount, start date, next payment, and eventually their offboarding status.

Nobody specifically asked me to build all of that. I saw an opportunity to make the process more organized and efficient, researched how to do it, and built something that made the information easier to manage.
